Support

Stuck on a puzzle, found a bug, or have an idea? Email isammadi@bargainbinstudio.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, the chapter you're in and what you were doing when it happened.

Quick answers

  • Controls: swipe up or down on the view to step, left or right to turn, and tap the view to use things. The arrow buttons beside the message line step sideways to dodge. Tap a weapon in a cat's hand to attack; a cat holding an orb or holy symbol opens the rune stickers instead, so you can cast spells. The pause menu (top left) has How to play and Controls.
  • Too hard or too easy? Pause → Difficulty switches between Cosy, Normal and Brave at any time.
  • Stuck on a puzzle: Granny's notes are kept in the Journal (pause menu). Read them again; every puzzle has a hint nearby.
  • Saves: the game autosaves and keeps three save slots on your device. Deleting the app deletes its saves.
  • Smoothness: if your phone runs warm, open the pause menu and set Effects to light.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Kawaii Kitten Krawler is a single-player game. We do not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.
  • The game works fully offline and makes no network requests. Its art, fonts and music are all inside the app.
  • Your saves and settings stay on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us.
  • If you email us for support, we use your email only to reply to you and delete it when the conversation is over.
  • The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: isammadi@bargainbinstudio.com.
